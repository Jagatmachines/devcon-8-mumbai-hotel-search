Properties west of the railway line are listed separately.

**How to read the price columns**

- **Both columns show ₹ (✔ Verified)** — Google Hotels listed rate plans with and without breakfast. The figures are the lowest room-only and lowest breakfast-inclusive rates across OTAs.
- **₹X in 'No Breakfast' plus the notice** — lowest rate found. No breakfast-inclusive plan was listed online.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property Google tags *Free breakfast*. Rate plans weren't exposed, so confirm breakfast is included.
- **Not priced online** — no bookable rate on Google Hotels for these dates. 'Contact property' means Google shows phone-only booking.
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates. Walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min. Each row shows the nearest station of any kind, the nearest of the other type, and the **distance to Bandra Colony Metro**.

## Analyst's Pick — Super Hotel O by OYO, Khar East (Saibaba Road)

- **Price:** ₹2,209 per night, lowest room-only rate. Google lists its breakfast as *chargeable*, but the add-on price isn't published online, so **Breakfast pricing unavailable online - confirm at front desk.**
- **Transit:** the closest bookable budget room to the target. It's **0.55 km (~9 min walk) to Bandra Colony Metro** and 0.42 km (~7 min walk) to Khar Road station (Western/Harbour), so you have both metro and suburban rail on foot.
- **Rating:** 4.4★ (178 reviews), among the highest of any in-budget property in the catchment.
- **Risk:** a minority of reviews report OTA bookings not being honoured, or cash-only payment despite assurances. Call the property to confirm the booking and payment method before you arrive.

**Confidence:** *High* on location and transit. *Medium* on price (one-date snapshot). *Low* on breakfast cost (not published).

**Alternates by use case**

- **Same distance, better rating:** Hotel O by OYO (formerly Kharsubway). ₹2,217, 4.5★ (264), 0.95 km to Bandra Colony Metro and 0.65 km to Khar Road station.
- **Cheapest walkable room:** Anil Guest House (Golibar). ₹1,644 room-only (Google tags it *free breakfast*, but no breakfast plan was priced online), 0.60 km to the metro. Only 3.2★ (422), so expect basic.
- **Confirmed breakfast price:** Hotel Arma Court (Bandra East, near Bandra station). ₹1,995 room-only vs ₹2,494 with breakfast (verified; about ₹500 extra). It's 1.7 km from Bandra Colony Metro but 0.44 km from Bandra station.
- **Solo / dorm:** Town Hostel Mumbai (Vakola). ₹924 for 2 guests, 4.3★ (758), 1.2 km from Bandra Colony Metro, but **0.2 km from Santacruz Metro, one stop away on the same Line 3**. Forrest Hostels (4.5★, 0.6 km) is closer but over the cap at ₹3,486.

## Caveats

- Rates change daily; re-check your dates. Prices for properties seen in several searches use the lowest seen.
- 2-adult pricing was used. Dorm prices for 2 guests likely mean 2 beds.
- 79 of these 90 properties also appear in the Santacruz East or Kalina reports. Their figures are the same snapshot.
- KALINA DRDO Guest House, 0.77 km away, is a government guest house and is likely not open to the public.
