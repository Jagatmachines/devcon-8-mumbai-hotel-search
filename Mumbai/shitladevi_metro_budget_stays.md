# Budget Stays — Shitladevi Mandir Metro Station (Line 3 Aqua), Mahim

**Snapshot:** Google Maps (discovery + coordinates) and Google Hotels (aggregated OTA rates) pulled 25 Oct 2026. Rates are for **Mon 26 → Tue 27 Oct 2026, 1 night, 2 adults, taxes included**. Google displayed NPR; converted at the fixed NPR/INR peg (1.60). Same basis as the earlier area reports.

**Coverage:** 64 properties within 2.7 km of Shitladevi Mandir Metro (Mahim West). That is 46 within the standard 2.3 km plus an 18-property 'Edge' band (2.3–2.7 km) added to reach a useful sample, since Mahim itself has very little lodging. 50 are at or under ₹3,000 or have no online rate; 14 are excluded for exceeding the cap.

**The key finding:** only **7 properties are within 1 km of the station**, mostly small Mahim guest houses that aren't sold online. The closest bookable in-budget rooms are on the Mahim–Dharavi side (0.7–1.4 km). The bigger budget clusters are around **Bandra station** (1.7–1.9 km), **Dharavi T-Junction** (1.4 km), and **Dadar West / Sion** (2.1–2.7 km). Shitladevi is one Line 3 stop from Dadar Metro.

**How to read the price columns**

- **Both columns show ₹ (✔ Verified)** — Google Hotels listed rate plans with and without breakfast.
- **₹X in 'No Breakfast' plus the notice** — lowest bookable rate found. No breakfast-inclusive plan was listed online.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property Google tags *Free breakfast*. Rate plans weren't exposed, so confirm breakfast is included.
- **Not priced online** — no rate on Google Hotels for these dates.
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates. Walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min. Each row also shows the **distance to Shitladevi Metro**.

## Analyst's Pick — Hotel Raheja Residency (Navrang Compound, near Raheja Hospital)

- **Price:** ₹2,138 per night, lowest room-only rate. Breakfast isn't sold online, so **Breakfast pricing unavailable online - confirm at front desk.** There's no in-house restaurant; reviews say guests order in.
- **Transit:** the closest bookable budget room to the target. It's **0.71 km (~12 min walk) to Shitladevi Metro** and 0.49 km (~8 min) to **Mahim Junction station** (Western + Harbour lines), so you have metro and suburban rail on foot.
- **Rating:** 3.8★ (130 reviews). Rooms are small but generally called clean.
- **Risk:** several reviews complain about rude morning-shift reception staff, and the Dharavi-side location is functional rather than pleasant.

**Confidence:** *High* on location and transit. *Medium* on price (one-date snapshot). *Low* on breakfast (not offered online).

**Alternates by use case**

- **Worth stretching the budget:** Hotel Al Saudia. ₹4,394 (over the cap) with a free-breakfast tag and 4.5★ (67). It's only **0.27 km (~4 min)** from the station, and reviews highlight the owner and staff.
- **Confirmed breakfast price:** Hotel Arma Court (Bandra East). ₹1,995 room-only vs ₹2,494 with breakfast (verified), 0.44 km from Bandra station, but 1.8 km from Shitladevi.
- **Cheapest private room near rail:** Hotel Golden Plaza. ₹1,457, opposite Bandra station (0.15 km), 3.7★ (452).
- **Solo / hostel:** Happinest – The Backpackers Hostel (Bandra West). ₹2,146 for 2 guests, 4.6★ (509), 0.59 km from Bandra station. The cheaper BKC Corporate Hostel & Stays is ₹966 but rated 3.6★.

## Caveats

- Rates change daily; re-check your dates.
- 2-adult pricing was used. Dorm prices for 2 guests likely mean 2 beds.
- Several low-cost listings here carry reviews about bookings not being honoured or prices raised at check-in (flagged in the table). Call ahead.
- UREST, about 0.27 km away, is a women-only, long-stay-oriented residence, not a nightly hotel.
