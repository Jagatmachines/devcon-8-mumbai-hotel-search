## Promos
- **Zostel Passport Pro, ₹499/month.** Shown in the zostel.com booking summary.
  - Benefits: a monthly free dorm night and 10% off dorms.
  - Estimate if both apply to this booking: (4,396 − 1,099) × 0.9 × 1.05 + 499 ≈ ₹3,615.
  - The stacking, the auto-renewal and whether the free night is usable straight away are all unverified. Read the terms before you buy.
- **Zo app rewards ($Zo).** GrabOn lists these as valid until 31 Oct 2026. Their value isn't stated.
- **Coupon codes.** I found no verified, Mumbai-applicable Zostel code. GrabOn and DesiDime show only destination deals for other locations. Agoda and Cleartrip "codes" listed on aggregators don't reveal the string, and none could be verified for this property.
- **OTA codes.** MakeMyTrip codes seen on Zoutons (FIRSTHOMESTAY, MMTESCAPE) were marked expired on 4 Sep. I didn't find Zostel Mumbai on MakeMyTrip or Goibibo.

## Hacks
1. Book on zostel.com and pay in INR. Every OTA is ₹450–470 more expensive for the same dorm beds.
2. If your plans may change, Vio's refundable rate costs ₹469 more. That works out as about 10% insurance on a non-refundable booking.
