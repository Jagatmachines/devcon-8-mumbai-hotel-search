# Budget Stays — Santacruz East (Mumbai)

**Snapshot:** Google Maps (discovery + coordinates) and Google Hotels (aggregated OTA rates) pulled 25 Oct 2026. Rates are for **Mon 26 → Tue 27 Oct 2026, 1 night, 2 adults, taxes included**. Google displayed NPR; converted at the fixed NPR/INR peg (1.60). Same basis as the Marol Naka and Kalina reports.

**Coverage:** 70 properties within ~2.3 km of the Vakola / Santacruz East station area. 49 are at or under ₹3,000 or have no online rate; 21 are excluded for exceeding the cap. The far south-east edge overlaps the Kalina report (Kolivery Village / CST Road properties).

**Geofence note:** 7 properties just west of the railway line (Santacruz West / Khar West) are kept in a separate 'Across the tracks' section. They are within a 5-minute walk of Santacruz or Khar Road station but are not technically Santacruz East.

**Market reality:** Santacruz East is the priciest of the three areas researched. Only 27 properties showed an online rate at or under ₹3,000, versus 91 in Marol Naka. The core Vakola cluster's 3★ hotels (Legend, Accord, Midland, MyOtel, Regency) all price at ₹3,390–6,340.

**How to read the price columns**

- **Both columns show ₹ (✔ Verified)** — Google Hotels listed rate plans with and without breakfast. The figures are the lowest room-only and lowest breakfast-inclusive rates across OTAs.
- **₹X in 'No Breakfast' plus the notice** — lowest rate found. No breakfast-inclusive plan was listed online.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property Google tags *Free breakfast*. The detail page didn't expose rate plans, so confirm the plan includes breakfast.
- **Not priced online** — no bookable rate on Google Hotels for these dates.
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates. Walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min.

## Analyst's Pick — Hotel Green Park Rooms (Nehru Road, Vakola)

- **Price:** ₹2,109 per night, lowest listed rate. Google's listing tags it *Free breakfast*, so the cheapest plan is likely to include breakfast. Its detail page didn't show rate plans, so confirm when booking.
- **Transit:** about 0.36 km (~6 min walk) to **Santacruz Metro (Line 3 Aqua)**, which runs direct to the airport T1/T2, BKC and CSMT. Santacruz station (Western/Harbour) is about 0.9 km away.
- **Why it wins:** it's the best mix of an under-cap price, likely-included breakfast, a metro station within a 6-minute walk, and a reasonable rating (3.8★, 113 reviews).
- **Risk:** the review base is modest. The Vakola strip around Marble Lane draws complaints about drainage and mosquitoes, so read recent reviews.

**Confidence:** *High* on location and transit. *Medium* on price (one-date snapshot). *Medium* that breakfast is included in that rate.

**Alternates by use case**

- **Confirmed breakfast price:** FabExpress Orange Suites. ₹2,091 room-only vs ₹2,722 with breakfast (verified; about ₹631 extra), 0.23 km from Santacruz Metro. Its 3.2★ rating (824 reviews) is the weak spot.
- **Best-rated private room:** Hotel O by OYO (formerly Kharsubway). ₹2,217 room-only, 4.5★ (264), about 0.65 km from Khar Road station and 0.7 km from Santacruz Metro. Breakfast is not priced online.
- **Solo / dorm:** Town Hostel Mumbai. ₹924 for 2 guests (likely 2 beds), 4.3★ (758), 0.21 km from Santacruz Metro. The 4.6★ Nap Manor Hostels next door is ₹2,502†.

## Caveats

- Rates change daily; re-check your dates.
- 2-adult pricing was used. Dorm prices for 2 guests likely mean 2 beds.
- Hotel Golden Galaxy, Hotel Apple Inn and Hotel Prince Palace showed a price on the Google list, but their detail pages returned no bookable rate plans. Treat those prices as indicative.
- Hotel Galaxy (Prabhat Colony) shows 'Contact this property' on Google, so call for rates.
