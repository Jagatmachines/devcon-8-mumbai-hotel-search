# Budget Stays — Kalina, Santacruz East (Mumbai)

**Snapshot:** Google Maps (discovery + coordinates) and Google Hotels (aggregated OTA rates) pulled 24–25 Oct 2026. Rates are for **Mon 26 → Tue 27 Oct 2026, 1 night, 2 adults, taxes included**. Google displayed NPR; converted at the fixed NPR/INR peg (1.60). Same basis as the Marol Naka report, so the two are directly comparable.

**Coverage:** 110 properties within ~2.3 km of Kalina (University campus / Kolivery Village) found on Google Maps and Google Hotels; 94 at or under ₹3,000 or without an online rate, 16 excluded for exceeding the cap.

**Transit reality check:** Kalina's core has **no metro station within ~1.5 km**. The Metro Line 2B stations planned through Kalina (Kalina/ITO area) are still under construction; only Line 2B's Mandale–Chembur stretch is open. Usable transit sits at the edges: **Santacruz Metro (Line 3 Aqua) + Santacruz station** on the Vakola side, **Kurla station (Central/Harbour)** on the east, and **BKC / Bandra Colony Metro (Line 3)** to the south-west.

**How to read the price columns**

- **Both columns show ₹ (✔ in the Verified column)** — 16 properties where Google Hotels listed rate plans with and without breakfast. The figures are the lowest room-only rate and the lowest breakfast-inclusive rate across all OTAs. They may be different room types or OTAs, so treat the gap as an approximate breakfast cost.
- **₹X in 'No Breakfast' plus the notice in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest rate found. No breakfast-inclusive plan was listed online.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — the property-level detail page wouldn't load, so this is the lowest listed rate at a property Google tags *Free breakfast*. Confirm the plan includes it.
- **Not priced online** — no bookable rate on Google Hotels for these dates. Most dorms here take phone or WhatsApp bookings.
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates. Walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min. Both the nearest station (metro or rail) and the nearest station of the other type are given.

## Analyst's Pick — Hotel Royce Executive (CST Road, Kapadia Nagar)

- **Price:** ₹1,599 room-only vs ₹1,914 with breakfast. This is a verified, like-for-like comparison: the same Superior Queen Room on Agoda, so breakfast costs about **₹315** more. Both rates are well under the cap.
- **Evidence base:** 3.9★ from **2,237** Google reviews, the most-reviewed property in this area with an online rate under ₹3,000. Its rating is more reliable than the 4.5★ scores from a few dozen reviews nearby.
- **Location:** on CST Road about 1 km from Kalina's centre, so Kalina, BKC and the US Consulate are a short auto ride away.
- **Transit, the trade-off:** Kurla station (Central/Harbour) is about 1.2 km straight-line (~20 min walk). BKC Metro (Line 3) is about 2.6 km. No budget property in Kalina's core is within easy walking distance of a metro or railway station.

**Confidence:** *High* on the breakfast price gap (same room, same OTA, same date). *Medium* on the absolute price (one-date snapshot). *High* on location.

## Caveats

- Rates change daily; re-check your dates.
- 2-adult pricing was used because single-occupancy searches don't return rates. Dorm prices for 2 guests likely mean 2 beds.
- The verified ₹ gaps compare the cheapest plans, which can be different room types. Only Royce's comparison is confirmed to be the same room.
- Many BKC-fringe budget hotels (Motilal Nehru Nagar / Kolivery) draw recurring reviews about slum-adjacent access lanes, windowless rooms and pests. Read recent reviews first.
- **Diamond by Glitz Hotels:** recent reviews say the property is closed and online bookings were not honoured. Avoid it.
