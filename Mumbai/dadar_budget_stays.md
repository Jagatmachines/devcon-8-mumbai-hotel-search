# Budget Stays — Dadar (Mumbai)

**Snapshot:** Google Maps (discovery + coordinates) and Google Hotels (aggregated OTA rates) pulled 25 Oct 2026. Rates are for **Mon 26 → Tue 27 Oct 2026, 1 night, 2 adults, taxes included**. Google displayed NPR; converted at the fixed NPR/INR peg (1.60). Same basis as the earlier area reports.

**Coverage:** 55 properties within 2.3 km of Dadar station (Western + Central interchange): Dadar East/West, Naigaon, Hindmata, Shivaji Park, Matunga, Parel and Prabhadevi edges. 43 are at or under ₹3,000 or have no online rate; 12 are excluded for exceeding the cap.

**Market reality:** Dadar's budget supply is mostly **old-style lodges and guest houses that aren't sold online**. Only **12** in-budget properties showed a bookable online rate, and **none exposed separate rate plans with and without breakfast**, so no breakfast price could be verified here. Branded and 3★ hotels near the station (Avon Ruby, Bawa Regency, City Point, Kohinoor Park, Ramee Guestline) all price at ₹5,200–11,200. Expect to phone or walk in for most lodges.

**Transit:** Dadar is Mumbai's best-connected rail hub, with the Western and Central lines interchanging here. **Dadar Metro (Line 3 Aqua)** is about 0.7 km west of the station, and Siddhivinayak and Shitladevi metro stations serve the Prabhadevi and Mahim edges.

**How to read the price columns**

- **₹X in 'No Breakfast' plus the notice** — lowest bookable rate found. No breakfast-inclusive plan was listed online. 'Chargeable' means Google says breakfast is available for a fee.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property Google tags *Free breakfast*. Rate plans weren't exposed, so confirm breakfast is included.
- **'Indicative'** — Google's list showed a price, but the property's detail page had no bookable rate.
- **Not priced online** — no rate on Google Hotels for these dates. Most lodges here take walk-ins or phone bookings.
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates. Walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min.

## Analyst's Pick — Sharda Lodge (Dr Ambedkar Road, Dadar East)

- **Price:** ₹1,635 per night, lowest room-only rate. Google lists its breakfast as *chargeable*, but the add-on price isn't published, so **Breakfast pricing unavailable online - confirm at front desk.** It sits on the main road with many eateries next door, so a cheap outside breakfast is easy.
- **Transit:** about 0.56 km (~9 min walk) to **Dadar station**, where the Western and Central lines interchange. Dadar Metro (Line 3) is about 1.1 km.
- **Evidence base:** 3.4★ from **765** Google reviews, the largest review base among the cheapest bookable options.
- **Risk:** reviews note very small rooms and late-night noise from a nearby club. It suits a functional 1–2 night stay.

**Confidence:** *High* on location and transit. *Medium* on price (one-date snapshot). *Low* on breakfast cost (not published).

**Alternates by use case**

- **Breakfast likely included:** City Guest House (Dadasaheb Phalke Road). ₹2,700† with Google's *Free breakfast* tag, 3.5★ (675), about 0.33 km (~5 min) from Dadar station. Its detail page had no bookable rate, so phone to confirm the price and breakfast.
- **Cheaper and still walkable:** OYO 72150 Capital Guest House. ₹1,968, 0.48 km from Dadar station. Only 3.1★ (72).
- **West side / metro access:** Hotel Rooms Dadar. ₹2,728 (breakfast chargeable), 3.5★ (390), about 0.37 km from Dadar Metro (Line 3).

## Caveats

- Rates change daily; re-check your dates.
- 2-adult pricing was used.
- Many low-priced listings here have recurring complaints about tiny windowless rooms, chawl-style buildings and OTA bookings not being honoured. Read recent reviews and call ahead.
- The two Gadge Maharaj dharamshalas are charitable stays that mainly serve patients and families visiting Tata Memorial and KEM hospitals. They are not general tourist lodging.
