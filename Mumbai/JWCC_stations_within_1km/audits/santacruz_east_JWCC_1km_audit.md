## Scope, method and limits

- **Filter used (from your file):** budget ≤ ₹3,000 (or not priced) and ≤ 1 km straight-line from a metro station serving Jio World Convention Centre: **BKC Metro** or **Bandra Colony Metro** (Line 3 Aqua).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Google Maps:** every review was read live in the browser, sorted Newest, with review dates, reviewer review counts and owner replies. I computed rating mix, last-6-month mix and single-review-account share for each property.
  - **OTAs:** OYO, Hotels.com/Expedia, Hostelworld, Trip.com and Tripadvisor were checked directly. Booking.com and Agoda scores came via KAYAK where KAYAK lists the property. Booking.com and Agoda block direct reads, and most of these budget properties aren't listed on them at all, which I note per property.
- **Recency:** the last 3–6 months carry the most weight.
- **Deal-breaker pattern rule:** ≥2 independent reports in one category within ~12 months, ≥1 in the last 6 months → **Shady/Red Flag**. Where I deviate from the strict rule, the section says why.
- **Confidence:** High = dated recent reviews across 2+ sources agree. Medium = clear evidence with a recency or sample gap. Low = thin or stale evidence.
- Distances and prices are copied from your file. Google "x months ago" dates are relative to 26 Oct 2026.
