# Hotel Audit — Shitladevi Metro Station

Source report: `shitladevi_metro_budget_stays.md` (64 properties within 2.7 km). Audit date: 25–26 Oct 2026.

## Scope & method

**What I audited:** the report's Analyst's Pick, its four alternates, and Hotel Triton. Triton was the highest-rated property in budget in the screen.

**Evidence:**
- The 10–20 most recent Google reviews for each property, from Google Hotels, including review dates and owner replies.
- The Google Maps review sample.
- Booking.com and Agoda scores (via KAYAK) and Skyscanner scores, where available.

**How the verdicts were set:**
- Reviews from the last 6 months carry the most weight.
- A deal-breaker counts as a pattern when there are at least 2 independent reports within about 12 months, at least 1 of them in the last 6 months. A pattern means **Shady/Red Flag**.

**Data gap:** Google Hotels had no review page for Hotel Raheja Residency, so its review dates can't be verified.

### 1. Hotel Raheja Residency (Analyst's Pick)

- **Verdict:** Proceed With Caution. Confidence is low because the reviews are undated.
- **The Reality Check:** It's the closest bookable budget room to the station, at 0.71 km. However, an aggregator that pulls in OTA scores rates it 2.6/5 against Google's 3.8, and Maps reviewers describe hostile morning-shift staff and photos that don't match the rooms.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** Each item below has only one report, and none can be dated, so none meets the pattern rule.
  - **Bait-and-switch photos:** one reviewer said the pictures are fake and the rooms are below what's shown.
  - **Odours:** rooms smelled bad, and only the AC masked it.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** Two separate reviewers describe rude morning reception staff. In one case, a receptionist started shouting when asked for a one-hour late checkout.
- **Green Flags:** Some reviewers call the rooms well kept and good value for solo travellers. It's near Raheja Hospital and 0.49 km from Mahim Junction.
- **Management Assessment:** There are no owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google rates it 3.8/5 (130 reviews). Skyscanner's aggregate is 2.6/5 (113), labelled "below average", drawn from providers including Agoda, Booking.com and Trip.com. That's a significant gap.

### 2. Hotel Al Saudia (Mahim West)

- **Verdict:** Proceed With Caution
- **The Reality Check:** It's 0.27 km from the station and has a 4.5★ Google score. But the reviews show strong signs of being manufactured, and its handful of Booking.com and Agoda ratings average 4.5/10, with cockroach complaints.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** None in the dated Google sample. The OTA summary on KAYAK mentions cockroaches and cleanliness complaints, but these are undated and come from 6 ratings.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - **Review authenticity:** a 5★ review from 10 months ago begins "Here is a WhatsApp style note you can send", which is AI-generated text pasted in unedited.
  - Many short 5★ reviews arrive in clusters: four at "11 months", three at "10 months", three at "8 months" and three at "6 months".
  - One 1★ review from about a year ago reports paying ₹3,800 for a Standard AC room and calls it the worst experience they've had.
  - It's over the ₹3,000 cap.
- **Green Flags:** Specific praise for the owner, Mr Shabbir, and the location (Hinduja Hospital and the metro on foot). Guests describe it as a family hotel.
- **Management Assessment:** There are no owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:**

  | Platform | Score | Reviews |
  |---|---|---|
  | Google | 4.5/5 (≈9/10) | 67 |
  | Agoda | 5.3/10 | 5 |
  | Booking.com | 1/10 | 1 |
  | KAYAK overall | 4.5/10 | 6 |

  Both OTA samples are tiny, so read them as a warning sign, not proof.

### 3. Hotel Arma Court (Bandra East)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag. The full audit is in the Bandra Colony file.
- **The Reality Check:** Its location scores 9/10 on OTAs, but its overall score on both Booking.com and Agoda is 4.7/10. Guests report a broken door lock, dirty linen and noise. The Google positives are a cluster of "contact Sunil sir" reviews.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - A room door without a proper lock (7 months ago).
  - Dirty linen and bathrooms (recurring on OTAs).
  - A noisy area (7 months ago and on OTAs).
  - A booking cut from 3 nights to 2 on arrival (11 months ago).
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** No lift, narrow corridors, and a cluster of solicited-looking reviews.
- **Green Flags:** 0.44 km to Bandra station, a restaurant downstairs, and a breakfast price that was verified.
- **Management Assessment:** There are no owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google 3.3/5 versus Booking.com 4.7/10 (120) and Agoda 4.7/10 (30).

### 4. Hotel Golden Plaza (opposite Bandra station)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** It's directly across from Bandra station. OTA guests rate its cleanliness 3/10 and say the rooms don't match the photos. No Google review in the last 6 months says anything substantive.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Severe Cleanliness:**
    - 8 months ago: unclean rooms and rude staff.
    - KAYAK cleanliness score: 3/10 across 62 OTA reviews.
    - About 2 years ago: mosquitoes.
  - **Bait-and-switch:**
    - KAYAK flags complaints that the room differs from the advertised photos.
    - A 2-year-old review says the rooms looked fancy on booking apps but were "a nightmare" in reality.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** One guest a year ago and another 4 years ago report being refused at the desk on religious or nationality grounds, even though the hotel takes bookings on foreign sites. Other problems:
  - Steep stairs.
  - Gates locked at night.
  - No visitors allowed.
- **Green Flags:** Prime location, and a long-term repeat guest (8 years) calls it pocket-friendly.
- **Management Assessment:** There are no owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:**
  - Google: 3.7/5 (452)
  - KAYAK: 5.3/10 (62), with cleanliness 3/10 and room size 2.5/10
  - Tripadvisor: 2.3/5

### 5. Happinest – The Backpackers Hostel (Bandra West)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** Its 4.6★ rests on many warm reviews from solo travellers. But the 1★ reviews from the last 1–5 months describe a hostile owner, rats, and a guest asked to leave before the end of a paid stay. The owner's own replies confirm the hostility.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Deceptive Management (hostile replies):** the owner's public replies have insulted reviewers. Examples:
    - Calling them "spoiled brats" whose parents "didn't teach you manners" (5 months ago, posted twice).
    - Lecturing a parent about their daughter's behaviour and clubbing (1 month ago).
    - Mocking guests who "book just because it's cheap" (2 months ago).
  - **Deceptive Management (eviction):** 4 months ago, a long-stay guest says they were told to leave 11 days before the end of a paid stay, apparently for higher-paying guests.
  - **Severe Cleanliness:** 4 months ago, rats, plus smoking despite no-smoking signs. The owner's reply says pest control was then introduced.
  - **Noise:**
    - 6 months ago: plaster walls, so noise travels.
    - 1 month ago: staff drinking and making noise at night.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** A unisex washroom that wasn't disclosed in advance (2 months ago). The strict cancellation policy (48 hours) is enforced combatively.
- **Green Flags:** Many detailed 5★ reviews from solo and female travellers who felt safe. Good social atmosphere, and 0.59 km to Bandra station.
- **Management Assessment:** This is the worst in the audit. Some replies are gracious, but several are personal attacks on reviewers. That is an explicit red flag under the brief.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** No OTA aggregate was found. Google: 4.6/5 (509).

### 6. Hotel Triton (Sion) — extra

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** It has a high Google score (4.6★, 849) and many clean-room reviews. But within the last 6 months, two separate guests report money problems: staff demanding extra cash, and an advance payment that wasn't returned.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** **Deceptive Management (hidden fees / payments):**
  - 6 months ago: staff "consistently demand extra money in cash" and became rude when questioned.
  - 4 months ago (Tripadvisor): an advance-booking payment was stuck, and the hotel didn't respond to several calls.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** It's 2.51 km from Shitladevi Metro, at the edge of the report's zone.
- **Green Flags:** Detailed recent praise for very clean rooms and washrooms and quick check-in. It's 0.16 km from Sion station, and a reviewer mentions shuttle buses nearby.
- **Management Assessment:** Positive reviews get one copy-paste template ("Hello Sir, we're glad to hear that you had a fantastic stay…"). The reply to the cash-demand complaint is formal and defensive ("However, after reviewing…").
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google: 4.6/5 (849). Tripadvisor: 3.5/5 (2). No Booking.com or Agoda aggregate was found.

## Revised recommendation for Shitladevi Metro

- **None of the audited properties earns "Genuine Top Choice."**
- **If you need to be near Shitladevi:** Hotel Raheja Residency remains the pragmatic pick. Book it refundable, ask to see the room before paying, and avoid early-morning check-in or checkout negotiations with the front desk.
- **If you can stay about 2.6 km away:** City Guest House in Dadar (₹2,700†, Proceed With Caution; see the Dadar audit) is the more reliable option. Its scores are consistent across five platforms, it's 0.33 km from Dadar station, and Shitladevi is a short ride away.
- **Avoid:** Happinest, despite its 4.6★, because of the owner's documented hostility to guests.
