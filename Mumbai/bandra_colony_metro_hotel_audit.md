# Hotel Audit — Bandra Colony Metro Station

Source report: `bandra_colony_metro_budget_stays.md` (90 properties). Audit date: 25–26 Oct 2026.

## Scope & method

- **Properties audited:** the report's Analyst's Pick and its four alternates, plus Nap Manor Hostels. Nap Manor is in this report as #17 and was the strongest candidate found in the screen.
- **Evidence:**
  - The 10–20 most recent Google reviews for each property, with dates and owner replies, from Google Hotels.
  - The Google Maps review sample.
  - Booking.com and Agoda scores, through KAYAK.
- **Recency:** reviews from the last 6 months carry the most weight.
- **Pattern rule:** a deal-breaker category counts as a pattern when there are at least 2 independent reports within about 12 months, with at least 1 in the last 6 months. Any pattern results in **Shady/Red Flag**.

### 1. Super Hotel O by OYO Khar East (Analyst's Pick)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** Its 4.4★ comes mostly from one-line 5★ reviews. The detailed reviews from the past year repeatedly describe pre-paid bookings that weren't honoured and payment terms that changed on arrival.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Deceptive Management (lost reservations):**
    - 3 months ago: after an OYO booking was paid, the hotel said it didn't exist in its system, claimed it was full and refused a refund.
    - 8 months ago: a booking made two months earlier, with a promised early check-in, had no room available on arrival.
  - **Bait-and-switch:**
    - 10 months ago: the guest was told by phone that card payment was accepted, then told on arrival that it wasn't.
    - 9 months ago: the rooms didn't look like the photos.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** There's no lift. The washrooms are very small. On one 8 am arrival the door was locked because the person inside was asleep.
- **Green Flags:** It's 0.42 km from Khar Road station and 0.55 km from the metro. A few recent reviews praise polite staff.
- **Management Assessment:** No owner replies, so the booking complaints go unanswered.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** No Booking.com or Agoda aggregate was found. Many of the 5★ reviews are short and generic, for example "Great Hotel Clean rooms and Politely staff i would like to have Give 5 Star ratings". That points to an inflated Google score.

### 2. Hotel O by OYO (formerly Kharsubway)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag. The full audit is in the Santacruz East file.
- **The Reality Check:** It's rated 4.5★, but the detailed recent reviews describe cockroaches, no hot water, and a room that only became available after the guest escalated to Airbnb.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - Cockroaches: 2 months ago and 8 months ago.
  - No hot water: 2 months ago. No water: 10 months ago.
  - Lost reservation, plus photos that didn't match reality: 10 months ago.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** The surrounding area made one guest uncomfortable.
- **Green Flags:** The receptionist and the location get praise.
- **Management Assessment:** No owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** No OTA aggregate was found. The recent one-line 5★ reviews look inflated.

### 3. Anil Guest House (Golibar)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** This is the clearest failure in the area. Three separate 1★ reviews from 3 months ago describe no water, no working AC or heater, dirty rooms and rude staff. Older reviews report cockroaches in the bedding.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Infrastructure:**
    - 3 months ago, ×3: frequent water shortages; the heater and AC not working; a room that smelled like a dustbin.
  - **Severe Cleanliness:**
    - 6 months ago: bedsheets full of insects and cockroaches.
    - 7 months ago: a filthy room full of mosquitoes.
    - 10 months ago: dirty sheets and floor.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - 7 months ago, staff refused a passport or masked Aadhaar and insisted on the full Aadhaar number. Be careful about handing over ID details.
  - The room has no window. Google's "free breakfast" tag is unverified.
- **Green Flags:** One 4★ review from 5 months ago calls it calm and quiet inside a housing society.
- **Management Assessment:** No owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google rates it 3.2/5 from 422 reviews. No OTA aggregate was found.

### 4. Hotel Arma Court (Bandra East)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** It's very close to Bandra station. Booking.com and Agoda both score it 4.7/10, and almost half of its 161 OTA ratings are "Poor". Its Google positives are dominated by near-identical reviews telling guests to "contact Sunil sir".
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Safety:**
    - 7 months ago: the room door didn't have a proper lock.
    - 6 months ago: a reviewer marked safety as "No" and described a slum-adjacent setting.
  - **Severe Cleanliness:** dirty bed linen and unclean bathrooms recur in Booking.com and Agoda reviews (KAYAK cleanliness score 4.0/10). 7 months ago, a guest reported a smelly room.
  - **Infrastructure / noise:**
    - 7 months ago: a noisy area.
    - The OTA summary notes early-morning street noise and noise from a nearby religious site.
    - About 1 year ago (Trip.com): no hot water.
  - **Lost reservation:** 11 months ago, a 3-night booking was cut to 2 nights on arrival.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - **Review authenticity:** at least five 4–5★ reviews from the last 4–9 months all tell guests to contact "Sunil sir". That's a classic solicited-review cluster.
  - There's no lift, the corridors are very narrow, and towels and soap have to be requested.
- **Green Flags:**
  - The location scores 9/10 on OTAs.
  - There's a restaurant downstairs.
  - The ₹500 breakfast premium was verified.
- **Management Assessment:** No owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:**

  | Platform | Score | Reviews |

  | Google | 3.3/5 (about 6.6/10) | 458 |
  | Trip.com | 3.4/5 | 8 |
  | Booking.com | 4.7/10 | 120 |
  | Agoda | 4.7/10 | 30 |

### 5. Town Hostel Mumbai

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag. The full audit is in the Kalina file.
- **The Reality Check:** Bed bugs were reported 3 months ago and rats on the bed 2 months ago. Booking.com reviews repeatedly complain about bugs and bad-smelling water.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** Bed bugs, rats, and luggage storage that can't be locked.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** The walk-in price is negotiated at the desk.
- **Green Flags:** It's 0.2 km from Santacruz Metro and the staff are helpful.
- **Management Assessment:** Every review gets the same copy-paste reply, including the bed-bug report.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google 4.3/5 vs Booking.com 6.4/10 (274) and Agoda 4.9/10.

### 6. Nap Manor Hostels (report #17)

- **Verdict:** Genuine Top Choice
- **The Reality Check:** Scores of 4.5–4.6 are consistent across Google, Trip.com and Tripadvisor, and the recent reviews are detailed. It's 1.37 km from Bandra Colony Metro, but only 0.36 km from Santacruz Metro, which is one stop away on the same Line 3.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** None detected.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** There's one defensive owner reply from 2 months ago, which cites CCTV footage in response to a complaint about how a health emergency was handled.
- **Green Flags:** Clean private ensuite rooms and dorms, hot water, social events, and repeat guests.
- **Management Assessment:** Replies are personalised.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** None. The scores are consistent (Google 4.6/735, Trip.com 4.5/41, Tripadvisor 4.6/20).

## Revised recommendation for Bandra Colony Metro

- **Every walkable shortlisted property failed the audit**, including the Analyst's Pick.
- **Best option:** Nap Manor Hostels (₹2,222† for 2 guests). Ride one metro stop, or walk about 22 minutes, to Bandra Colony.
- **If you need a private hotel room within walking distance:** none of the audited properties is safe to recommend. Forrest Hostels (4.5★, 0.6 km) is the next candidate to check, but it is over the cap at ₹3,486 and was not audited here.
