# Hotel Audit — Dadar

Source report: `dadar_budget_stays.md` (55 properties, of which only 12 had a price). Audit date: 25–26 Oct 2026.

## Scope & method

I audited the Analyst's Pick, the three named alternates, and Sai Sharan Dormitory, which the report had flagged.

**Evidence used:**
- The 10–20 most recent Google reviews for each property, from Google Hotels, with review dates and any owner replies.
- Google Maps review samples.
- Ratings from Tripadvisor and Trip.com.
- Booking.com and Agoda scores (via KAYAK) and Hotels.com scores.

**How verdicts were decided:**
- Reviews from the last 6 months carry the most weight.
- A deal-breaker counts as a pattern when there are at least 2 independent reports in the same category within about 12 months, with at least 1 of them in the last 6 months.
- Any pattern means **Shady/Red Flag**.

**Data gap:** Google Hotels had no review page for OYO 72150 Capital Guest House, Hotel Rooms Dadar or Sai Sharan Dormitory. Their audits rely on Google Maps review samples, which have no dates, so I couldn't verify how recent those complaints are.

### 1. Sharda Lodge (Analyst's Pick)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** Several recent reviews are honest 4★ write-ups from people who wanted a basic room near the station. Recent 1★ reviews, though, report cockroaches and guests who booked an AC double online but were given a non-AC room with a fan.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Deceptive Management (bait-and-switch room type):**
    - 5 months ago: the room was listed online as an AC double, but on arrival the guest got a plain room with a fan.
    - 10 months ago: staff showed one room before booking and then said that room type wasn't available.
  - **Severe Cleanliness:**
    - 4 months ago: cockroaches in the room.
    - 10 months ago: dirty towels that staff wouldn't change.
  - **Infrastructure:**
    - 10 months ago: the AC wasn't working properly.
    - About 1 year ago: the power tripped at night, with short-circuit sounds from the AC.
    - Undated Maps review: loud club music nearby late at night in a dirty, cramped room, which cost ₹5,000.
    - On Hotels.com, rooms are described as divided by plywood or plastic partitions (paper walls).
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - Front-desk rudeness comes up again and again over 2 years.
  - There is no lift; the building has 3 floors.
  - An older review mentions smoke smell in the corridors.
- **Green Flags:**
  - A detailed 4★ review from 3 months ago (a stay on 15/07/2026) found the toilet clean with hot water, the AC cooling well, and complimentary towels and water provided.
  - It's on the main road, 0.56 km from Dadar station.
- **Management Assessment:** No owner replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:**
  - Google: 3.4/5 (765)
  - Trip.com: 2.1/5 (3)
  - Tripadvisor: 2.3/5 (6)
  - Hotels.com: 2.8/10 (6), listed as "Sharda Residency" at the same address, 188 Dr Ambedkar Road
  - Google is the most generous of these.

### 2. City Guest House (Dadasaheb Phalke Road)

- **Verdict:** Proceed With Caution, the best of the Dadar shortlist.
- **The Reality Check:** Its scores are close across every platform, which is a good sign the reviews are honest. No review from the last 6 months on Google reports a deal-breaker. OTA guests do keep raising weak AC and stuffy, sometimes windowless rooms.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:** None detected in the last 6 months. The only borderline item: KAYAK's Booking.com and Agoda summary lists weak or non-working AC and poor ventilation as recurring complaints, but those reviews are undated.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - Rooms are not soundproof.
  - There is no lift.
  - The entrance is dimly lit with no signboard, and one guest reported a bad smell at the building entrance.
  - The breakfast has limited options.
  - Two recent 5★ reviews name the same staff member, "Shashi/Sashi", which suggests mild solicitation.
- **Green Flags:**
  - Recent reviews (1–5 months old) consistently describe clean rooms and washrooms, good AC, and helpful staff.
  - Two women travelling alone said they felt safe.
  - A Goibibo booking with complimentary breakfast was honoured.
  - It's 0.33 km from Dadar station.
- **Management Assessment:** The owner replies to every review, but with one identical template: "Thank you for choosing our hotel, and for taking the time to write this detailed review…". The same reply goes even to a lukewarm 3★ review. It's harmless, but generic.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:**
  - Google: 3.5/5 (675)
  - Booking.com: 6.3/10 (219)
  - Agoda: 7.2/10 (45)
  - Trip.com: 3.4/5 (103)
  - Tripadvisor: 3.5/5 (29)
  - The scores are consistent.

### 3. OYO 72150 Capital Guest House

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag. Confidence on recency is low because the Maps reviews have no dates.
- **The Reality Check:** All 5 Maps reviews are negative, and each describes a different failure: a stinking room, unclean sheets, refilled water bottles, and a gate locked at night.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Severe Cleanliness:** a stinking room with no ventilation that needed air freshener to be usable (a cover-up odour); unclean bedsheets; very dirty towels.
  - **Infrastructure:** poor hot water; the AC switching off on its own.
  - **Deceptive Management:** a "common bathroom" that didn't match what the guest expected; staff demanding a ₹100 tip.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:**
  - Unsealed water bottles, refilled from a local source.
  - The gate is locked at night; a guest says the receptionist and guard abused them for ordering food delivery.
- **Green Flags:** One reviewer said the rooms themselves were "better" than the service.
- **Management Assessment:** No replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google: 3.1/5 (72). No OTA aggregate was found.

### 4. Hotel Rooms Dadar (N C Kelkar Marg)

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag. Confidence on recency is low because the Maps reviews have no dates.
- **The Reality Check:** Reviews are split. Some guests got a clean, small room near the station and the metro. Others describe bed bugs, no ventilation and a foul smell.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Severe Cleanliness:** bed bugs and mosquitoes, with no help from staff; a filthy bathroom; a constant foul smell in a room with no ventilation.
  - Two independent negative reviews out of 5.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** Staff were rude. The entrance is hard to find, with no clear directions.
- **Green Flags:** Some guests had neat rooms with hot water, a smart TV and AC, and named helpful staff. It's 0.37 km from Dadar Metro (Line 3).
- **Management Assessment:** No replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google: 3.5/5 (390). No OTA aggregate was found.

### 5. Sai Sharan Dormitory

- **Verdict:** Shady/Red Flag
- **The Reality Check:** This dormitory crams about 30 beds into roughly 450 sq ft. Several reviewers report bed bugs and staff who switch your fan off from the reception desk.
- **Deal-Breakers Found:**
  - **Severe Cleanliness:** bed bugs ("khatmal") with bite marks after the first night; insects in the bed, reported separately; unwashed bedsheets.
  - **Infrastructure:** no AC, fan restrictions, no charging points, and no ventilation.
- **Other Critical Red Flags:** ₹500 charged for 4 hours. The receptionist is rude. One guest reported losing money.
- **Green Flags:** None that matter.
- **Management Assessment:** No replies.
- **Cross-Platform Discrepancies:** Google: 2.8/5 (106). It's not on the OTAs checked.

---

## Revised recommendation for Dadar

- **Pick:** City Guest House (₹2,700†, 0.33 km from Dadar station) replaces Sharda Lodge.
  - It's the only audited Dadar property without a recent deal-breaker, and its scores agree across five platforms.
  - Call ahead to confirm the price and breakfast, and ask for a room with a window and good AC.
- **Sharda Lodge:** only if budget is the overriding concern. Insist on seeing the room before paying, and confirm in writing that it has AC.
