## Method notes

- Line 3 distance is taken from the source row: the primary station if it is L3, otherwise the 'nearest metro' value (all such stations in these reports are L3).
- Where the source gives only a 0.1 km-rounded value, 0.9 or less is kept, 1.1 or more is removed, and 1.0 is borderline — unless a precise 2-decimal figure exists in the Bandra Colony or Shitladevi report for the same property, in which case that is used.
- **Line 1-only rows (inferred):** for properties whose nearest metro is Airport Road, Saki Naka or Chakala (Line 1), the Line 3 distance was estimated by locating each property from its two source distances (to the L1 station and to Andheri Rly) against Wikipedia station coordinates (Marol Naka, Airport Road, Saki Naka, Chakala, Sahar Road) and a published coordinate for MIDC–Andheri, then cross-checked with the source's 1 km Marol Naka geofence (core zone = within 1 km). Ranges reflect the two-point ambiguity and 0.1 km rounding. Confidence: **Medium**. MIDC–Andheri's coordinate comes from a transit-directory site, not Wikipedia, so the five Chakala-side keeps are the least certain (**Medium-Low**).
