# Hotel Audit — Kalina, Santacruz East (Line 3 ≤ 1 km shortlist)

Input: `kalina_L3_1km.md` (17 properties: 17 within 1 km + 0 borderline). Audit date: 26 Oct 2026. Stay: Mon 26 → Tue 27 Oct 2026, 2 adults, ≤ ₹3,000.

## Scope, method and limits

- **Every property in the input file is audited below.** Properties already audited on 25–26 Oct are carried over from those audit files (marked *prior audit*); the rest were researched today.
- **Platforms checked:** Google Maps/Google Hotels, Booking.com, Agoda (directly or via KAYAK/Skyscanner/Trivago aggregates), plus OYO, FabHotels, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Tripadvisor, Hotels.com/Expedia, Trip.com, ixigo and Hostelworld wherever the property is listed.
- **Recency rule:** reviews from the last 3–6 months carry the most weight.
- **Deal-breaker pattern rule:** ≥2 independent reports in one category (cleanliness, safety, deceptive management, infrastructure) within ~12 months, with ≥1 in the last 6 months → **Shady/Red Flag**.
- **Confidence tiers:** High = dated recent reviews on 2+ platforms agree; Medium = solid aggregate scores or partly dated evidence; Low = thin, undated or single-source evidence.
- **Known gap:** this run hit the per-turn web-search cap partway through. Properties marked *thin evidence* rest mainly on their Google rating plus whatever OTA listing was found; they are **not** cleared. Many are phone/walk-in lodges, PGs and dorms with almost no footprint on Booking.com or Agoda, which is itself a risk (no platform recourse if the booking isn't honoured).
