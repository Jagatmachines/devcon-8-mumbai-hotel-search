**Coverage:** 132 properties within ~2.3 km of the Marol Naka junction found on Google Maps/Google Hotels; 127 at or below ₹3,000 (or with no online rate), 5 excluded for exceeding the cap. 4–5★ hotels near the junction (Radisson Blu, Novotel, Kohinoor Continental, Tunga International, etc.) were dropped as out of budget before pricing.

**How to read the price columns**

- **₹X in 'No Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property whose Google listing marks breakfast as *paid* or doesn't say it's free, so the rate is treated as room-only. The 'With Breakfast' column then carries the required notice.
- **₹X† in 'With Breakfast'** — lowest listed rate at a property whose Google listing says *Free breakfast*. Google tags breakfast at the property level, not per rate plan, so † means: confirm when booking that the plan includes breakfast.
- **Treebo Prince** is the only property where a rate-plan-level split was verified (brand site room-only vs. Agoda/EaseMyTrip breakfast-inclusive).
- **Not priced online** — no bookable rate shown on Google Hotels for these dates (Google shows 'Contact this property' for some dorms).
- **Distances** are straight-line from Google Maps coordinates to the nearest metro station; walking time assumes a 1.3× route factor at 80 m/min. The nearest suburban railway station for this whole area is **Andheri (Western/Harbour)**, 2–5 km west, listed for each property.

## Analyst's Pick — Hotel KC Residency (Chimatpada, Marol)

- **Price:** ₹1,686 per night, lowest listed rate (2 adults, taxes in). Its Google listing says *Free breakfast*. That makes it the cheapest private-room property with a free-breakfast tag within 100 m of the interchange. Confirm the plan includes breakfast when you book.
- **Transit:** about 60 m (~1 min walk) from **Marol Naka Metro**, the only interchange in the area. It gives you Line 1 (Versova–Ghatkopar) and Line 3 Aqua (direct to Airport T1/T2, BKC and CSMT) without a rickshaw.
- **Why it wins:** it has the best combination of low price, a free-breakfast tag and the shortest walk to the metro among properties with a meaningful review base (3.7★ from 817 Google reviews).
- **Risk:** reviews repeatedly mention small, windowless rooms and a dated building. It suits a functional 1–3 night stay, not a long one.

**Confidence:** *High* on location and transit (geocoded). *Medium* on price (a one-date snapshot). *Low–medium* that breakfast is included in that exact rate.

## Caveats

- Rates change daily. Re-check your own dates on Google Hotels or the OTA before relying on them.
- 2-adult pricing was used because single-occupancy queries returned no rates. Dorm prices for 2 guests likely mean 2 beds.
- Unpriced dorms and PGs (e.g. Sheetal, Marol Metro, Dormitory World, Metropolis, AFRA, Aqua Marol) are often bookable only by phone or WhatsApp. Typical rates are not quoted here to avoid guessing.
- Ratings come from Google Maps. Several low-priced properties have strongly negative recent reviews (hygiene, misleading photos). Read reviews before booking anything under 3.5★.
